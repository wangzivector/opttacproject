OptTac-integrated Universal Manipulation Interface

Materials and instructions for building the OptTac-integrated Universal Manipulation Interface.

Hardware assembly

1. Mechanical components

The UMI exoskeleton parts includes four groups, PLA 3D-printed with STL at OptTac_Manufacture:
1. hand base: Tag cube and hand base [palm_print folder]
2. thumb: the thumb exploded view [thumb_print folder]
3. index finger: the finger exploded view [finger_print folder]
4. middle finger: the finger exploded view [finger_print folder]
For index and middle fingers, the length of distal phalanges are adjustable by choosing different screw holes. The approx phalanges for two fingers are different, with individual STL files for each finger.

2. Tag alignment

Electronic assembly

1. Electronic components

UMI: Three primary hand fingers are equipped with OptPads controlled by the Exci-Meas module which is integrated into the marker cube of the visual fiducial system. The thumb is articulated by four-degree-of-freedom angular encoders, while each of two affiliated fingers is kinetically encoded by three joints.
